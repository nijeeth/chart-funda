# What's New

User-facing highlights for the current release of Chart Funda. Unlike `CHANGELOG.md`, this file is rewritten for each release and shows only the latest version. It is the source for the "What's new" text in the Chrome Web Store listing.

## 2.0.0

- **New Corp. Filings tab** — recent NSE corporate filings for the charted stock, each with its category chip (orders, results, resignations, M&A, insider trades…) and a link to the official NSE PDF.
- **Custom Fish RS Rank (Experimental)** — your stock's Fish RS rank with 1W/1M/3M/6M history, shown at the top of the Filings tab and as a small chip in the panel header.
- **Feed freshness rows** — the Filings tab shows the feeds' own update times, green when fresh and red past 48 hours.
- **Back button** — after clicking a peer to jump charts, one tap returns to the original stock.
- **Trendlyne link** — a third button beside Screener and Tijori opens the company's Trendlyne page.
- **Earnings banner now shows the actual date** alongside the quarter.
- **Click-anywhere minimize** — new dashboard toggle (on by default): clicking the chart collapses the panel to the pill.
- Refresh button moved to the panel header — always reachable, including on errors.
- Faster, smarter fetching — filings feeds are only downloaded when their timestamp actually changes.
- New icons.

## 1.0.0

- Fundamental panel on TradingView charts for NSE and BSE stocks: price, market cap, P/E, P/B, 52-week high and low, dividend yield, ROCE, and ROE.
- Quarterly growth figures with the period they came from, plus a line showing whether the quarter on screen is current.
- Strengths and concerns from the company page.
- Shareholding for Promoters, FIIs, DIIs, Government, and Public, with change since last quarter.
- Peers with price, P/E, and market cap. Click a peer to switch the chart.
- Earnings date from Trendlyne, colored by how near it is.
- Consolidated or standalone results toggle, light and dark theme, classic and compact pill — each choice is remembered.
- Dashboard with an optional Chartink redirect, a panel on/off switch, and curated market links.
